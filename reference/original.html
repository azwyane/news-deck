About

News Deck — a client-side RSS reader with on-device AI summaries and a built-in article reader. Articles are fetched via CORS proxies and the Jina Reader API. Your reading data stays on this device. For Ollama: OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* ollama serve

Install

  1. Tap the browser's menu (Chrome) or share icon.
  2. Choose "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
